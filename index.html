Baylo

Mobile app · barter & trade marketplace with AI matching
Dart (Flutter)FirebaseSQLite

Baylo is a mobile marketplace built for bartering instead of buying — list an item, browse what others are offering, and let AI suggest fair trade matches based on item value. It covers the full loop: sign up, post a listing, get matched, send a trade offer, and message the other party until the trade is complete.
